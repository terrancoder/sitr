Sitr settings

Everything on this page is stored in your browser. Your browsing — pages you visit, searches, blocked requests — never leaves your device. If this device is part of a Family household, the household settings below (never your browsing) sync between your devices as an end-to-end-encrypted blob our server cannot read.

Filter categories

Adult-content filtering and SafeSearch are always on — they are what Sitr is for. Optional categories:

    Images & media on mixed-content sites

    Blocks images and media by type, on top of the blocklist. Sitr cannot tell what a blocked image showed — images on affected sites will simply look broken. That is this setting working.

    Show images on these sites

    Sites where images and media stay visible in allowlist-only mode. Blocklisted domains stay blocked regardless.

      Just this once

      Show images on a site until the browser closes — nothing is written to disk, and the exception disappears on restart.

        Always allow these sites

        Opens a site that a category blocks, except adult sites. Use for false positives — and please also report them (below) so the public blocklist is fixed for everyone.

          Always block these sites

          Blocked in addition to the blocklist.

            Report a site

            Ask to block a site · Report a wrongly blocked site · Email instead

            These open GitHub or your mail app. A GitHub report is public and needs a GitHub account. Sitr itself sends nothing.

            Wait before loosening

            When this is on, a change that loosens protection is held: you ask for it, wait, and then have 10 minutes to make it. Changes that tighten protection apply straight away. This slows down changes made inside Sitr; it cannot stop someone disabling or removing the extension in the browser, and changing the computer's clock defeats it. The wait is local to this browser: changes made on another device in your household arrive by sync and are not held. For a child's device, use a household with a guardian PIN instead.

            Household

            Share allow/block lists and settings across your family's devices. Everything stays on your devices — if sync is used, the server only ever stores an encrypted blob it cannot read.
