# Sitr brand kit

The logo is the **shield emblem alone**: a shield drawn as a twisted ribbon of
fine green and gold lines (supplied by the owner, made with Canva). The
product name is **Sitr**, unchanged. Everything here except `source/` and `palette/` is
generated by `npm run build:icons` (`node tools/icons/render-brand.mjs`); do
not edit generated files by hand.

## Name and voice
- **Sitr** — سِتْر, "covering · shielding · protection". Domain: sitrshield.com.
- Store name: "Sitr — Halal & Family Content Filter".
- The product name is "Sitr". Where a graphic shows the name next to the logo,
  it is the "Sitr سِتْر" name lockup set in the graphic's own fonts, as before.
- Tagline: "A content filter you can audit." Header line: "a content filter that is *private by construction*".
- Publisher: Dooplin Apps.
- Claims rule (from `extension/store-listing/listing.md`): no absolutist claims
  ("zero data collection"); only specific, substantiated ones.

## source/
The logo files supplied by the owner (made with Canva), with their embedded
metadata removed before publishing: the author name, account identifiers and
Canva's content-credentials record. The image data is unchanged (see
`source/SOURCE.txt`).
`Sitr Shield Logo without background.png` (1264 px) is the master; every
logo asset is derived from its emblem. The owner's image also carries the
words "Sitr Shield" under the emblem: by the owner's decision of
19 November 2026 those words are not used anywhere. `Sitr Shield Logo.svg` is
not a vector (it wraps two embedded PNGs): never ship it as artwork.

## Rules
- **Which file for which size.** Where the emblem is drawn more than 48 px
  tall, use the owner's artwork (`logo/sitr-shield-emblem*.png` and the
  icons). At 48 px and below the fine lines turn to mush, so use the
  **small mark** (`logo/sitr-shield-small*.svg` / `-16…48.png`): solid facets
  traced from the artwork. The generator switches automatically. Never use the
  artwork at 48 px or below, or the small mark above 64 px.
- **No enlarging.** The emblem is about 800 x 860 px in the master; do not
  scale it above 110 % of that. Make it smaller in the layout instead.
- **Ground.** The artwork is drawn for white or light paper. Icons always sit
  on white (#ffffff). On dark grounds use either the `-dark` files (the same
  artwork recoloured: green #3aa886, gold #c9a558) or the light emblem on a
  white rounded tile; never the light files directly on dark.
- **Clear space.** Keep at least 1/4 of the emblem's width clear on every side.
- **Minimum size.** 16 px (small mark).

## preview.png
Every generated asset at real size on its usual ground, from `preview.html`.

## Regenerate
`npm run build:icons` (everything, including the app icons, extension icons,
Android launcher layers, Play and Chrome Web Store graphics) or
`node tools/icons/render-brand.mjs <steps>` with any of `masters icons web
store social preview`. It needs a Chromium: `SITR_CHROME`, else `BRAVE_PATH`,
else Brave in /Applications. No dependencies; output is byte-identical
between runs on the same browser build.
